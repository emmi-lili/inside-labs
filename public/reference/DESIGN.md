## Brand & Style
This design system captures an autonomous, high-velocity AI and growth intelligence platform. Built for performance marketers, founders, and revenue operators, it rejects sluggish enterprise design in favor of sharp, kinetic utility and high-clarity telemetry.

The visual direction fuses **Light-Mode Cyber-Minimalism** with subtle **Frosted Precision Glassmorphism**:
- **Pure Canvas Radiance:** Crisp, pure white (`#FFFFFF`) and ultra-light cool slate surfaces (`#F8FAFC`) create an uncluttered backdrop that accentuates high-density analytics and rapid signal extraction.
- **Electric Growth Signifiers:** Vibrant algorithmic greens signify operational uptime, compounding revenue conversions, and real-time AI autonomous execution.
- **Micro-Futuristic Density:** Translucent glass tiles, micro-indicator status dots, subtle emerald glow drops, hairline boundary borders, and deep charcoal-to-black typography establish authority and high-tech craft.

## Colors
The palette balances ultra-luminous canvas whites, stark Obsidian ink for typography, and vibrant energetic kinetic greens for live signal paths.

### Primary Accents & Signals
- **Primary Energy (`#00D06C`):** High-saturation hyper-growth green. Used for key call-to-actions, live chart paths, affirmative state badges, and dynamic conversion highlights.
- **Tertiary Stable Emerald (`#10B981`):** Deep kinetic green for subtle hover states, icons, secondary trends, and glowing pill markers.
- **Green Luster Tint (`#ECFDF5`):** Soft, mint-infused surface wash for highlighted cards, banners, and pill backdrops.

### Structural Neutrals & Contrasts
- **Deep Obsidian (`#090D16` / `#0F172A`):** Pure contrast ink. Used for headlines, dense data summaries, high-tech dark pill badges, and contrast floating chips (e.g. dynamic pill toggles).
- **Subdued Slate (`#64748B` / `#94A3B8`):** Informational hierarchy text, micro-labels, axis legends, and inactive UI borders.
- **Surface Canvas (`#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`):** Base workspace background, ensuring all translucent layers and neon green drop glows remain sharp and legible.

## Typography
The system unifies the structural geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans** with the technical data cadence of **Space Grotesk**.

- **Editorial & Narrative:** Headlines use tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.035em`) to pack impact into growth value statements. Key programmatic terms (such as automated action outcomes) leverage inline color fills with the vibrant `#00D06C` tone.
- **Telemetry & Key Indicators:** `Space Grotesk` is strictly reserved for live agent performance metrics, percentage deltas (`+42%`, `1.2k +Leads`, `<9s`), status trackers, and monospaced category tags.
- **Hierarchy Rules:** High-tier badges and section overlines must be rendered in `label-mono` with uppercase transforms to indicate algorithmic sub-systems.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a 12-column responsive fluid grid with generous vertical macro-spacing to emphasize airy white-space and focused telemetry cards.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, maximum container width of `1280px`, anchored with `3rem` screen margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` gutters, `2rem` screen margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, `1.25rem` margins. Complex composite metrics reflow from horizontal nodes into vertically stacked floating glass modules.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm:** Component interiors operate on an 8px base rhythm (`space-sm` for badge interiors, `space-md` for standard card padding, `space-xl` to `space-3xl` for high-impact section tiers).

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is constructed through semi-translucent layering, subtle refraction borders, and energetic localized halos rather than heavy opaque drop shadows.

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Flat `#F8FAFC` or `#FFFFFF` foundation.
- **Level 1 (Glassmorphic Card Deck):** `#FFFFFF` with `0.85` alpha, `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, bounded by a `1px` translucent border of `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`. Shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Telemetry):** `#FFFFFF` with `0.95` alpha, bordered by `1px` solid `rgba(0, 208, 108, 0.2)`. Shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`, combined with a localized ambient glow: `0 0 24px rgba(0, 208, 108, 0.15)`.
- **Level 3 (Dark Anchor Modules & Overlays):** Solid `#0F172A` with micro-glass reflection (`1px inset rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`). Deep drop shadow: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.25)`.
- **Halo Energy Accents:** Radial background gradients behind hero graphics: `radial-gradient(circle at 50% 50%, rgba(0, 208, 108, 0.12) 0%, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0) 70%)`.

## Shapes
A balanced medium-rounded aesthetic (`roundedness: 2`) gives components an approachable yet engineered feel.

- **Cards & Data Panels:** `1rem` (`rounded-lg`) corner radii for structured cards, giving them an architectural, display-like quality.
- **Pills & Status Indicators:** Full capsule curves (`9999px`) are applied strictly to category markers, interactive badges, avatar nodes, and primary action buttons.
- **Floating Controls & Inputs:** `0.75rem` radius to seamlessly match adjacent glass cards.
- **Node Connectors:** Fine `1.5px` dashed paths (`#A7F3D0` / `#00D06C`) using organic spline bends to represent algorithmic pipelines connecting agent nodes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Kinetic Action:** Fully rounded pill button (`rounded-full`), solid `#00D06C` background, crisp pure white `#FFFFFF` text in `label-ui`. On hover: subtle scale `1.02` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -2px rgba(0, 208, 108, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary Ghost Action:** Pill shape with a hairline border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`), pure white backdrop, text in `#0F172A`. On hover: `#F8FAFC` background with a `#00D06C` border accent.
- **Inline Text Trigger:** Dark label paired with a small directional arrow (`→`) in `#00D06C`, sliding right on hover by `4px`.

### Badges & Status Indicators
- **Agent Active Pill:** Light emerald fill (`#ECFDF5`), `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.2)`, `label-mono` text in `#047857`. Left-anchored by a pulsing live green beacon: `w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-[#00D06C]` with an outer ping animation.
- **Dark Control Capsule:** Jet Obsidian pill (`#0F172A`) containing white monospace metrics or toggle switches (`bg-[#00D06C]`).

### Metric & Glass Cards
- **Telemetry Card:** Frosted translucent backing (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`), `16px` padding, displaying mini sparklines in `#00D06C` alongside large `metric-md` figures.
- **Step Card (Process Flow):** White card with a centered or top-left numbered indicator badge (`32x32px` circular tint of `#ECFDF5` with `#00D06C` text). Contains a high-contrast headline (`headline-sm`) and muted slate body copy.

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- **Search & Prompt Field:** Pill-shaped or `0.75rem` rounded input container with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)` fill, slate placeholder (`#94A3B8`), and an active focus state defined by a `1px` ring of `#00D06C` and a diffuse outer shadow.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- **Toggle Switches:** Oval track with smooth transition. Inactive: `#E2E8F0`; Active: `#00D06C`. Thumb is a pure white circular elevation disc.
- **Checkboxes:** `6px` radius squircle. Checked: `#00D06C` fill with a white vector checkmark.
